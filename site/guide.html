How InvestEd works

A quick guide to the parts of the app that people ask about most.

The learning path

InvestEd has 12 units with 5 lessons each. Lessons unlock in order. When you finish a unit's lessons, its Knowledge Check unlocks. Pass it to open the next unit.

Not sure where to start? The placement test asks a few questions and drops you at the right unit. You can retake it anytime in Profile > Settings.

Hearts

You start every lesson with 5 hearts. A wrong answer costs one heart and shows you the explanation. Run out and the lesson restarts, so slow down and read the explanation.

XP, levels, and Gold

  • XP: 20 per lesson, plus a 10 XP bonus for a perfect lesson. Every 100 XP is a new level.
  • Gold: 10 per lesson (15 if perfect) and 25 for passing a Knowledge Check. Spend it in the Shop on avatar gear and Streak Freezes.
  • Gold is only earned, never bought, and it has no cash value.

Streaks and Streak Freezes

Finish at least one lesson a day to grow your streak. A Streak Freeze (150 Gold, hold up to 3) automatically covers one missed day. You can turn the evening reminder on or off in Settings.

The practice portfolio

You start with $10,000 of practice money.

  • Prices are real. Quotes come from the stock market, delayed by a few seconds.
  • Trades are not. Nothing you buy is real and nothing can be cashed out.
  • Search any ticker on the Market tab, then use Buy or Sell on its page.
  • Portfolio value = your cash + what your stocks and options are worth right now.

Options, simply

An option is a bet on where a stock's price will be by a certain date.

  • A call pays off if the price goes up past the strike.
  • A put pays off if the price goes down past the strike.
  • The premium is what you pay for the bet. It's the most you can lose.
  • Breakeven is the price the stock must reach at expiry for you to get your premium back.

InvestEd prices options with the Black-Scholes formula at 30% volatility. Real option prices vary. This is for learning.

Friends and weekly leagues

Add friends by username or by sharing your friend code. Everyone's weekly XP puts them in a coin league that climbs from Penny to Nickel, Dime, Quarter, Silver Dollar, and Fortune. Leagues reset every week.

Other people can see only your username, avatar, level, and XP, never your email or portfolio.

Staying safe

Tap anyone in your friends list, the league board, or search to Report or Block them. Blocked users disappear from your leagues and can't send you requests. We review every report within 24 hours.
