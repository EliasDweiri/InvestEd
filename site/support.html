InvestEd Support

Need help with InvestEd? Email us at investedapp.help@outlook.com and we'll get back to you, usually within a day or two.

Common questions

Is any of this real money? No. Every trade in InvestEd uses practice money. Prices are real, but nothing you buy or sell is real, and you can't cash anything out.

I forgot my password. On the Log In screen, tap "Forgot password?" and we'll email you a reset link. If it doesn't arrive, check your spam folder or email us.

How do I delete my account? In the app, go to Profile > Settings > Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, progress, practice portfolio, and friend connections. If you can't sign in, email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.

How do I block or report someone? Open their profile from your Friends list or the league board and tap Block or Report. You can also email us with their username. We review every report within 24 hours.

How do I turn off streak reminders? Go to Profile > Settings and switch off "Daily streak reminder," or turn off notifications for InvestEd in your iPhone's Settings.

Is this financial advice? No. InvestEd is an educational app. Nothing in it is financial or investment advice. Talk with a licensed professional before investing real money.
