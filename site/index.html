Quick answers

Is any of this real money?

No. Every trade uses practice money. The prices are real, but you can't deposit, withdraw, or cash out anything.

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscriptions, and nothing to buy. Gold is earned by learning and can't be purchased.

Who is it for?

Teens 13 and up, and anyone who wants a friendly start with money and investing.

Is this financial advice?

No. InvestEd teaches concepts and lets you practice. Talk with a licensed professional before investing real money.
